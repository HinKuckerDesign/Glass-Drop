## How to Play 🎮🍺

### Power On
When the device is powered on, it starts in **IDLE mode**.
The display shows the currently selected game mode.

---

### Selecting a Game Mode
While in **IDLE**:

- **Short button press** → switch between modes
  - **H1** = Classic mode (fixed start)
  - **H2** = Random mode (random start delay)

The LEDs indicate the selected mode.

---

### Starting a Round
To start the game:

- **Hold the button for ~3 seconds**

The countdown will begin.

⚠️ **Important:**  
The glass must be placed on the coaster during the countdown.  
Lifting the glass too early results in an instant **LOSE**.

---

### Countdown
The game counts down:

- **3 → 2 → 1**
- then **GO**

In **H2 (Random mode)**, there will be an additional random delay before **GO** to increase tension.

---

### Reaction Phase
- The timer starts at **GO**
- Lift the glass as fast as possible
- The timer runs while the glass is lifted
- Place the glass back on the coaster to stop the timer

Your reaction time is shown on the display.

---

### Game Result
- **FINISHED** → valid time recorded
- **LOSE** → false start (glass lifted too early)

The time stays visible until the next round is started.

---

### Starting the Next Round
After **FINISHED** or **LOSE**:

- **Short button press** → start the next round
- The game keeps the selected mode

---

### Sound (Buzzer)
The buzzer can be enabled or disabled at any time during the game.

- **Hold the button during a game** → toggle sound **ON / OFF**
- The display briefly shows the sound status

🔇 This is useful if you want to play quietly or late at night.

---

## Notes & Tips
- For best results, place the glass flat on the coaster
- Transparent glasses with water are supported
- The proximity sensor automatically adapts to the environment
- The game is designed for fast reactions – false starts are part of the challenge 😉
